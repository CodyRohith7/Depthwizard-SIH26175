DEPTHWIZARD — 2.5 MINUTE EXPLAINER

0:00–0:12 HOOK
“One image. One scene. One measurement. DepthWizard turns a single optical image into depth, height and a navigable 3D environment.”

0:12–0:35 PROBLEM
“Traditional elevation products often depend on stereo, LiDAR or radar. A single image is agile, but the hard problems are scale ambiguity, remote-sensing domain gap and incomplete geometry.”

0:35–1:00 ARCHITECTURE
“DepthWizard begins with image-quality and scene understanding. A metric-capable monocular model provides a depth prior. Geometry then reasons about ground, roofs, planes and structural edges. Physical evidence anchors scale.”

1:00–1:25 TRUST
“The key difference is reliability. We don't return a naked number. We track depth reliability, scale reliability and geometric consistency. When evidence is insufficient, the system says so.”

1:25–1:50 MEASURE
“Select a building or structure. The system reconstructs its 3D points, estimates its relationship to the ground plane and returns a height with provenance and uncertainty.”

1:50–2:10 FLY
“Now the static image becomes a navigable 3D scene. Observed geometry, inferred geometry and unknown regions are kept distinct.”

2:10–2:30 PROOF
“We benchmark depth error, height error, reconstruction quality, uncertainty calibration and runtime, and use ablation studies to prove which modules matter. DepthWizard is not a depth viewer. It is a measurement pipeline from one image to a trustworthy 3D experience.”
